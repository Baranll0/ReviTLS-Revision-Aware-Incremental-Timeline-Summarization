\caption{Overview of \textbf{ReviTLS}. Phase~1 extracts structured claims with a $7\text{B}$ model, clusters routine same-day claims, and queues claims whose revision cue $\rho$ fires (lexicon, LLM flag, or structural probe). Phase~2 replays the stream chronologically: cached routine transitions are re-applied in order, and each queued claim is re-retrieved, reranked, and verified by a $14\text{B}$ model against the state and evidence available at its publication time only. A \texttt{RETRACTED} label is accepted only with a verbatim source quote. The cross-encoder reranks any shortlist with more than one candidate in both phases.}
